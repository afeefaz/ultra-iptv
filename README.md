# Ultra IPTV

A free IPTV player for Android phones, Android TV and Fire TV. No ads and no accounts. It doesn't include any channels or content: you add your own IPTV playlist.

## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/afeefaz/ultra-iptv-releases/releases/latest/download/UltraIPTV.apk)**

This link always gets the newest version. On Android TV or Fire TV, enter it in the Downloader app.

Android asks you to allow installs from the app you download with, and Play Protect may warn about an unknown app.
